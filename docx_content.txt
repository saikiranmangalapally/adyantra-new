⬛ FAQ — AI &amp; AUTOMATION
Q: Do we need to be a technology company to benefit from AI automation?
A: Absolutely not. Our most impactful AI deployments have been for traditional businesses — clinics, real estate agencies, retailers, and educational institutions — where even basic automation creates enormous time savings.
Q: How long does it take to deploy an AI chatbot or agent?
A: A WhatsApp AI chatbot for customer support or lead qualification can be live in 2–4 weeks. More complex systems involving custom AI models, CRM integration, and multi-step workflows take 6–10 weeks depending on scope.
Q: Will the AI understand our industry-specific language and context?
A: Yes. We train every AI system on your specific business data — your product catalogue, FAQs, past conversations, and brand guidelines — so it speaks your language, not a generic one.
Q: What happens when the AI gets something wrong?
A: Every system we build has confidence thresholds and human escalation protocols. If the AI is not confident in a response, it automatically routes the conversation to a human agent. You always stay in control.
Q: How much does AI automation cost?
A: Costs depend on complexity, integrations, and scale. A basic WhatsApp chatbot starts from ₹50,000 one-time. Full AI automation suites for mid-size businesses range from ₹2L–₹10L+ depending on scope. Every engagement begins with a free strategy session to estimate ROI before you commit.

⬛ FAQ — BRANDING &amp; CREATIVE SERVICES
Q: Do you only do logo design or the full brand identity?
A: We offer both — standalone logo design and full brand identity systems. We strongly recommend the full identity (logo + color system + typography + visual language + guidelines) because a logo alone doesn&apos;t create a coherent brand experience across all your marketing channels.
Q: How is a brand identity different from a logo?
A: A logo is one element. A brand identity is the complete visual system: the logo, the color palette, the typography, the icon library, the photography style, the pattern language, and the rules for how all of these work together. A brand identity is what makes your brand instantly recognizable across every touchpoint.
Q: Do you handle ongoing social media content creation?
A: Yes. We offer monthly creative retainers for social media — covering feed posts, stories, reels, and ad creative — with a dedicated design team who understands your brand and maintains consistency at scale.
Q: Can you redesign our existing brand without losing brand equity?
A: Yes. Brand evolution (improving an existing brand while preserving recognition) is different from a full rebrand. We start by auditing what brand equity exists, what to preserve, and what to improve — then execute an evolution or rebrand based on that evidence.
Q: What does a brand guidelines document include?
A: Our brand guidelines include: logo usage rules (sizes, clearspace, don&apos;ts), color system (primary, secondary, neutral palettes with HEX, RGB, CMYK values), typography system (font hierarchy, sizes, weights, usage), photography and illustration direction, icon usage, and co-branding rules.
